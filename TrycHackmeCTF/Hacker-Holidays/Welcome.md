# Welcome to the Hacker Holidays CTF Writeups 🎄

Welcome to the **Hacker Holidays CTF** writeups repository!

This repository contains writeups and solutions for the challenges from the **TryHackMe Hacker Holidays CTF event**. The writeups are intended for learning, reference, and helping others understand the techniques and concepts used in the challenges.

> **Note:** These writeups were uploaded **after the event had ended** and are intended strictly for educational purposes. They do not provide solutions during the active competition.

Each writeup explains the approach taken, tools and techniques used, and the steps involved in solving the respective challenge.
